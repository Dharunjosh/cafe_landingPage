Our story

Started with one oven and a stubborn idea.

  • Beans roasted in small batches, 12km from the counter
  • Cakes and biscuits baked in-house each morning, nothing frozen or trucked in
  • A table held for you whether you stay ten minutes or three hours
  • An excellent, pleasant atmosphere designed for lingering, not rushing
  • Service that's first in quality, every single visit

Brand story

Hearth & Bramble opened in a converted bicycle shop with one oven, four tables, and a name borrowed from the blackberry bramble that still grows along the back wall.

Six years on, the oven's the same one. So is the habit of saving the last seat by the window for whoever's had the longest week.

We're not chasing a second location. We're chasing the version of this one where every regular's order is already half-made before they reach the counter.
